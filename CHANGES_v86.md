# v86 - Clearer delta columns

- Delta and gap cells no longer inherit G-Sight significance background colours.
- Candidate score cells keep their significance colours.
- Percentage-point differences are displayed as rounded whole points: `+5 pts`, `-3 pts`, `0 pts`.
- Mean differences keep the decimal precision selected by the CMI.
- Configured positive and negative thresholds still apply bold green/red font styling to the delta value.
- The rules apply to both Monadic benchmark gaps and Paired candidate-minus-benchmark deltas.
