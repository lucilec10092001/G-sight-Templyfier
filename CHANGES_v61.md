# Templyfier v61

## Paired studies beyond adjacent product pairs

- Keeps automatic Benchmark-then-Candidate pairing when every split has a clear even product plan.
- Opens a small optional mapping only when a split cannot be paired safely or the CMI chooses to correct the proposal.
- Supports non-adjacent and reversed products, odd product counts with unused extras, different mappings by split, and one shared benchmark compared with several candidates.
- Shows which products are excluded before saving a manual mapping.
- Stores mappings by source workbook and worksheet so consolidated studies do not mix split settings.
- Blocks generation when a Paired mapping is missing or invalid instead of silently pairing the wrong products.

The Monadic engine, question detection, metric recipes and Excel structure remain unchanged.
