# Changes v96

- Coloured section headers are now opt-in instead of imposed by automatic detection.
- The CMI reviews the editable Section header column and activates Show section headers in Excel only when wanted.
- A blank Section header creates no coloured row and no automatic TOPLINES header.
- The recommended table clearly separates Section header from Item family, with examples and bulk-edit guidance.
- Item family continues to keep shared items such as Colour together under one variable independently of sections.
