# Templyfier v68

## Percentage gaps expressed in points

- Percentage-based gaps and Paired deltas are now displayed as `+X.X pts` or `-X.X pts`.
- The formula converts proportional G-Sight values into percentage-point differences.
- Mean differences retain their ordinary numeric format and selected decimal precision.
- Blank source comparisons remain blank.

## Configurable emphasis

- Output settings now offer an optional point-gap highlighting control.
- The CMI can choose the minimum positive-point difference shown in bold green.
- The CMI can choose the minimum negative-point difference shown in bold red.
- Default thresholds are +5 points and -5 points and can be disabled.

## Comfort Yellow regression

- `It smells better than my usual yellow fabric conditioner` is explicitly tested and remains available in all applicable splits, including TOTAL.
