# Changes v95

- Separated the two Excel concepts in the CMI journey: a Section creates a coloured header; an Item family groups several one-result items under one variable.
- Added Section header shown in Excel to the recommended question-table view.
- Added a visible Show section headers in Excel control.
- A single item family can no longer be split across several sections. Older contradictory configurations are reconciled into the section used by most family members.
- Members of one item family are written contiguously, even when their original question order was interrupted.
